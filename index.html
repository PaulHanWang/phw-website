Minneapolis Sailing Center

At the Minneapolis Sailing Center, I teach passionate students of all ages, of all skill levels. From complete beginners, to lifelong sailors, to advanced racers, I teach people from all walks of live how to improve their sailing skills, all while improving my own teaching and leadership skills.

Twin Cities Flight

At Twin Cities Flight, I took my learning to new heights (literally). By combining technical aeronautical knowledge, aviation regulations and legislation, and precise vehicle control in one of the most high-stakes, high-pressure environments on the planet, I reinforced my confidence, communication, and leadership skills.

Creative Arts

7 films, over 10 years, across 4 continents; I taught myself the ins and outs of cinematography. From home movies and road trip videos to wildlife photography and cinematic short films, my creative eye has given me a strong understanding of visual storytelling and the communication of information.

About Me

I study mechanical engineering at the University of Edinburgh and studied computer science, mechanical engineering, and aerospace engineering at the University of Minnesota for four years for my secondary school education. Finally, I am an amateur cinematographer, avid motorsports driver, and 70's classic rock enthusiast.
